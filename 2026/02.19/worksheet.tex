\noindent \textbf{Lesson Warmup:} (2022 AMC 10A, \# 15) Quadrilateral $ABCD$ with side lengths $AB=7, BC=24, CD=20, DA=15$ is inscribed in a circle. The area interior to the circle but exterior to the quadrilateral can be written in the form $\frac{a\pi-b}{c},$ where $a,b,$ and $c$ are positive integers such that $a$ and $c$ have no common prime factor. What is $a+b+c?$

\vspace{8em}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.5cm]
    \item (2022 AMC 10B, \#2) In rhombus $ABCD$, point $P$ lies on segment $\overline{AD}$ so that $\overline{BP}$ $\perp$ $\overline{AD}$, $AP = 3$, and $PD = 2$. What is the area of $ABCD$?

    \item In parallelogram $ABCD$, $AB=10$, $AD=7$, and $\angle BAD=30^\circ$. What is the area of $ABCD$?

    \item (2023 AMC 10A, \#4) A quadrilateral has all integer sides lengths, a perimeter of $26$, and one side of length $4$. What is the greatest possible length of one side of this quadrilateral?


    \item In convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, $\angle ABD=28^\circ$, $\angle CBD=17^\circ$, and $\angle ACD=28^\circ$.
    Find $\angle CAD$.

    \item Quadrilateral $ABCD$ satisfies $AB=AD$ and $CB=CD$.
    Given $AB=5$, $BC=3$, and $AC=6$, find the area of $ABCD$.

    \item (2024 AMC 10B, \#8) How many angles $\theta$ with $0\le\theta\le2\pi$ satisfy $\log(\sin(3\theta))+\log(\cos(2\theta))=0$? 

    \item Rectangle $ABCD$ has diagonal $\overline{AC}$ making angle $\theta$ with side $\overline{AB}$. If $\tan\theta=\frac34$ and the perimeter of the rectangle is $70$, find the area of the rectangle.

    \item (2024 AMC 12A, \#10) Let $\alpha$ be the radian measure of the smallest angle in a $3{-}4{-}5$ right triangle. Let $\beta$ be the radian measure of the smallest angle in a $7{-}24{-}25$ right triangle. In terms of $\alpha$, what is $\beta$? 

    \item In trapezoid $ABCD$ with $AB\parallel CD$, diagonals $\overline{AC}$ and $\overline{BD}$ intersect at $P$. If $AB=4$, $CD=10$, and $BP=6$, find $PD$.

    \item (2022 AMC 12A, \#12) Let $M$ be the midpoint of $\overline{AB}$ in regular tetrahedron $ABCD$. What is $\cos(\angle CMD)$?
