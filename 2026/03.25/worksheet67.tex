\noindent \textbf{Lesson Warmup:} (2025 AMC 8, \# 1) Determine, with proof, all positive integers $k$ such that\[\frac{1}{n+1} \sum_{i=0}^n \binom{n}{i}^k\]is an integer for every positive integer $n.$

\vspace{.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2cm]
    \item A factory produces 84,600 tennis balls each day. If they package 60 balls per box, and 60 boxes per truck, how many trucks do they fill each day?
    \item Sunshray is sails a large ship which counts the total number of miles travelled on a trip on its odometer. On the first day of a trip, he sails 1 mile, and each day after that, he sails twice as many miles as the day before. On what day during the trip will his odometer show 1111 miles?
    \item Find the last digit of $67^{41}$.
    \item Om is investing in the stock market. In his first year he has a 20 percent chance of striking rich. Each year after that the odds he strikes rich is increase by 25 percent. What is the probability that Om strikes rich in the third year?
    \item Srivatsan and Sreyas are sitting on a balanced seesaw. Srivatsan weighs 84 pounds and sits 24 inches from the fulcrum (the pivot point). Sreyas weighs 128 pounds. How far from the fulcrum must Sreyas sit for the seesaw to remain balanced?
    \item If Amon-Ra rolls a 6-sided die, a 7-sided die, and three 8-sided dice, what is the probability that the sum of all of the faces is even?
    \item What is the area(in terms of $\pi$) of a circle which has a circumference of 10 inches?
    \item In the International Football League which the Detroit Tigers play in wants to have each of the teams play seventeen games each. If there are 19 teams, can the IFL make the schedule?
    \item Rebanto the Writer writes all of the numbers from 1-226. How many times does Rebanto write the digit 2?
    \item Tanay thinks about some integer X whose factorial has 68 trailing zeros. Find the smallest integer X Tanay could be thinking of.
    \item (Westminster Problem) A convex quadrilateral ABCD has side lengths AB=8, BC=6, CD=26, and DA=24. The angle \angle ABC is $90^{\circ}$. What is the area of quadrilateral ABCD?
    \item Compute the number of ways in which an 67×1 grid can be fully tiled with rectangles of size 6×1 and 7×1.
    \item Convert 607 from base 10 to base 67.
    \item On March 14th, a spy eats a Pie. If the next time same spy eats a pie was on June 7th, how many days did the spy go without eating a pie? Keep in mind that on March 16th the spy went 1 day without eating a pie.
    \item Pipe A can fill a water tank in 2 hours, and Pipe B can fill it in 3 hours. Pipe C can drain the tank in 6 hours. If all three pipes are opened at the same time, how long in minutes will it take to fill the tank starting from empty?
    
\end{enumerate}
