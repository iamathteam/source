\section{Basic Practice}
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2.85cm]
    \item Sreyas was a very sneaky boy. One day, he decided to go scuba diving. He found 67 fish in the water and brought them home. However, he had only 12 fish tanks. If he placed x number of fish in each tank and had less than 12 left over, how many fish did Sreyas have left over?
    \item Soon Sreyas got bored of his fish. He decided to go to a skydiving location and mess around. There was a large pile of 111 parachutes. He decided to place them into 7 equal piles of maximum size. He ended up stealing the rest of the parachutes. How many parachutes did Sreyas Steal?
    \item Later in September, Sreyas started work at a sponge company. There were originally 676 sponges in a massive pile. The company produced packs of 31 sponges. Sreyas was able to secretly steal all of the sponges that were not packaged. How many packs were made and how many sponges did Sreyas steal?
    \item Sreyas like a sloth, slept many hours each week. He would sleep a total of 108 hours weekly. If he slept x hours some days and sleep one extra hour on other days, how many days did he sleep extra and how long did he sleep each day?
    \item Sreyas after all that stealing went to Jail. The judge and the jury were very bad people and wanted the sponges Sreyas stole. There are 12 people on the jury and they split the number of sponges Sreyas had. They split it evenly such that each juror revived X sponges and the rest(less than 12) were given to the judge. How many sponges did the Judge get?
    
    
\end{enumerate}

\section{Tougher Practice}
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep = 2.85cm]
    \item Sreyas started to spend his 7 years in jail. He was very terrible bored, and started to do terrible stuff with math. He found $2^{2198378}$mod(3) = X and did this by actually expanding that terrible long number. Can you figure out what value that Sreyas got for X?
    \item During the Superbowl all of the guards were distracted. Sreyas sneaks out but finds that he needs a code. He knows that the code was 2 digits and that it is 5 more than a multiple of 8 and 1 more than a multiple of 9. What is the code that would unlock the door?
    \item Sreyas successfully saved himself from the jail. He soon encountered a massive tennis ball warehouse. Each can has 3 balls each box has 20 cans. There are stacks 13 boxes high and there are 143 stacks. Sreyas dumps all the balls and creates 7 piles of balls size X.  Sreyas then stupidly steals the less than 7 remaining balls. How many balls does Sreyas steal?
    \item After this great adventure, Sreyas wants to go home. Since he stole the parachutes, he decides to hitch a ride on the Blackbird and jump off in Columbus, GA. He forgot that the plane was first going to take a stop in Ohio. He remembered that his watch gave a notification every 17 minutes. Once he got on the plane, he realized that it was a 4 hour and 19 minute flight. How much time will there be between Sreyas' watch's last notification before landing and the landing itself?
    \item Lockheed Martin is remaking many of their Blackbird planes to replace modern day cars. They have $607^2$ planes that they need to distribute to 41 companies. They will donate all leftover planes to IA Math Team. How many planes will they donate to us?
\end{enumerate}
