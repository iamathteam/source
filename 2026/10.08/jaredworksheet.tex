    \section{Problems}
    \item Jared the real rock roller has 10 identical real round rocks. He has 5 really roaring rooters that he wants to give the rocks to. How many ways can Rockin' Jared give out the rocks?
    \item The Michigan Wolverines play osu. If sum of the team's scores was 79, how many different scores are there for osu to lose the game? Please please please use stars and bars to solve this question.
    \item Stars and bars (balls and urns/boxes, etc) are used in relevant fields. Take the following example, an exercise in statistical mechanics. 
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Think of bosons as particles of interest, and they are all identical (if you're a physics nerd, this is Pauli exclusion principle).
        \item Each boson can have different discrete (``quantized'') energies $\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2,\varepsilon_4$.
        \item Think of the total number of microstates, $\Omega$, as how many distributions of bosons exist.
    \end{itemize}
    How many microstates can a system of 3 bosons have if there are only 4 energy levels?
    \item Goff the serious shark stored seven small identical schools of seafish. Goff sadly has only two friends. How many ways can Goff and his 2 friends eat the seven identical schools if each of them must receive at least one school of seafish
    \item Maintenance man Marty maintains 10 different mansions. Marty also mentors 3 minions. How many ways are there for Marty to give out the mansions to his minions.
