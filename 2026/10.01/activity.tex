\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=1.5em]
\item To retrieve Prince Martin Qi Ho from Supreme Commander Gadi, the quartermaster arranges an army a size between 60,000 and 70,000. To find the exact count, the army is tasked to arrange themselves in rows of 7, 9, 11, and 13 leaves, respectively: 3, 2, 1, and 3 soldiers are left over. Determine its exact size.

\item Five messengers intercepted values from Martin's secret quadratic code $f(x)=ax^2+bx+c$. Varun replaced exactly one value with a forgery. Identify the forged report and recover the secret $f(0)$
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
\text{Messenger }x&1&2&3&4&5\\ \hline
\text{Reported }f(x)&27&35&53&80&119
\end{array}
\]

\item Commander Varun's three watchtowers occupy A = (0, 0), B = (14, 0), and C = (5, 12). Commander Varun very evilley devises to figure out how many non-overlapping tanks with area 1 square unit will fit between the towers. What is the number Varun came up with?

\item Martin's rescue party consisting of Darth Vader and Greg Heffley must travel from (0, 0) to (6, 6) on the road network below. Every move is one unit north or one unit east. Enemy checkpoints at (2, 2) and (4, 4) cannot be visited. How many shortest routes remain?

\item The 8 knights of a round table sit in a circle. Battalion Commander Bangal and Sir Sabbani may not sit next to each other. How many arrangements are possible if rotations are considered identical, but reflections are considered different?

\item The final escape tunnel is a 2 $\times$ 9 grid of square rooms. Engineers can seal adjacent rooms with 1 $\times$ 2 rectangular barricades, placed either horizontally or vertically. Every room must be covered exactly once. How many different complete arrangements are possible?

\item Martin learns of new information that someone on his side betrayed him and stole his sword the MAGICALibur along with his Precious Ring.

Jannik Sinner: ``Gollum stole the sword.''

Gollum: ``Darth Vader stole the sword.''

Loki: ``I did not steal the sword.''

Darth Vader: ``Gollum is lying.''

Exactly one person stole the sword, and exactly one of the four statements is a lie. Who stole the trophy?

\item He was running out of time, he could see the sunlight up the sky, so he put his horse in overdrive, Marty. The city was very cold and empty and he could just barely glance at the analog clock before being blinded by the light. He could see that the angle between the hands was 89 degrees and the hour hand just past 7. Find the exact time of day.

\item Prince Martin is the kingdom's swiftest knight, completing the 50-mile Royal Jousting Circuit in just 2.75 hours. Two rival knights challenge him, requiring 4 hours and 5 hours, respectively, to finish. If the course loops around the tournament grounds in 1-mile laps, what lap will each rival be on when Prince Martin finishes?

\item Martin had taken a big kick from Marty* to the head when he was trying to setup his 2-seater horse. His horseodometer (in meters) started at 0 and ended at the number of pages in a diary of a wimpy book squared. The he realized he Forgot About Dre when Martin was getting on Marty while Dr. Dre was resting. If he goes back to get Dre and takes him to a tavern 500 km away, what number would his horseodometer show?

*Marty is Prince Martin's royal horse, the horse he was gifted for his 8th birthday. Royal horses can hit supersonic speeds.
\end{enumerate}
