Prince Martin is the kingdom's swiftest knight, completing the 50-mile Royal Jousting Circuit in just 2.75 hours. Two rival knights challenge him, requiring 4 hours and 5 hours, respectively, to finish. If the course loops around the tournament grounds in 1-mile laps, what lap will each rival be on when Prince Martin finishes?

\begin{solution}
Assume all three start together and each maintains a constant speed.
At $2.75=11/4$ hours, the rivals have traveled
\[
\frac{50}{4}\cdot\frac{11}{4}=\frac{275}{8}=34\tfrac38
\quad\text{and}\quad
\frac{50}{5}\cdot\frac{11}{4}=\frac{55}{2}=27\tfrac12
\]
miles. The first has completed $34$ laps and is on
$\boxed{\text{lap }35}$; the second has completed $27$ laps and is on
$\boxed{\text{lap }28}$.
\end{solution}
