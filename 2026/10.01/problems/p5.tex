Eight distinct officers sit around a circular table. Officers $A$ and
$B$ must not sit next to each other, nor may officers $C$ and $D$.
How many arrangements are possible if rotations are considered identical,
but reflections are considered different?

\begin{center}
\begin{asy}
size(4.3cm);
draw(circle((0,0),1.4),black+0.9bp);
for (int i=0; i<8; ++i) {
  real t=pi/2-i*pi/4;
  pair P=2.05*(cos(t),sin(t));
  draw(circle(P,0.24),black+0.9bp);
}
\end{asy}
\end{center}

\begin{solution}
There are $(8-1)!=5040$ circular arrangements in total. Treating
$A,B$ as one block, in either order, gives $2\cdot6!=1440$
arrangements with that forbidden adjacency; the same holds for $C,D$.
If both pairs are adjacent, there are $2^2\cdot5!=480$ arrangements.
Inclusion--exclusion gives
\[
\boxed{5040-1440-1440+480=2640}.
\]
\end{solution}
