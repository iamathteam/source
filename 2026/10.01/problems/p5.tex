The 8 knights of a round table sit in a circle. Battalion Commander Bangal and Sir Sabbani may not sit next to each other. How many arrangements are possible if rotations are considered identical, but reflections are considered different?

\begin{solution}
There are $(8-1)!=5040$ circular arrangements in total. Treating
Bangal and Sabbani as one block, in either order, gives $2\cdot6!=1440$
arrangements with the forbidden adjacency. Therefore the answer is
\[
\boxed{5040-1440=3600}.
\]
\end{solution}
