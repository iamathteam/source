Eight distinct knights sit around a circular table. Battalion Commander
Bangal and Sir Sabbani must not sit next to each other.
How many arrangements are possible if rotations are considered identical,
but reflections are considered different?

\begin{center}
\begin{asy}
size(4.3cm);
draw(circle((0,0),1.4),black+0.9bp);
for (int i=0; i<8; ++i) {
  real t=pi/2-i*pi/4;
  pair P=2.05*(cos(t),sin(t));
  draw(circle(P,0.24),black+0.9bp);
}
\end{asy}
\end{center}

\begin{solution}
There are $(8-1)!=5040$ circular arrangements in total. Treating
Bangal and Sabbani as one block, in either order, gives $2\cdot6!=1440$
arrangements with the forbidden adjacency. Therefore the answer is
\[
\boxed{5040-1440=3600}.
\]
\end{solution}
