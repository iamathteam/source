Martin's rescue party consisting of Darth Vader and Greg Heffley must travel from (0, 0) to (6, 6) on the road network below. Every move is one unit north or one unit east. Enemy checkpoints at (2, 2) and (4, 4) cannot be visited. How many shortest routes remain?

\begin{solution}
Every shortest route consists of six east and six north moves, so there
are $\binom{12}{6}=924$ without restrictions.
There are $\binom42\binom84=420$ routes through $(2,2)$ and,
symmetrically, $420$ through $(4,4)$. Routes through both checkpoints
were subtracted twice: their number is $\binom42^3=216$.
By inclusion--exclusion, the answer is
\[
\boxed{924-420-420+216=300}.
\]
\end{solution}
