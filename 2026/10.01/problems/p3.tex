Three of Varun's watchtowers occupy $A=(0,0)$, $B=(14,0)$, and $C=(5,12)$.
Intelligence places Martin's prison at a point equally distant from all
three towers. A secret tunnel entrance lies inside the triangle, equally
distant from its three sides. Find both locations and their common
distances from the towers and roads, respectively.

\begin{center}
\begin{asy}
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0), B=(14,0), C=(5,12);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,black+0.9bp);
dot(A,black+4bp); dot(B,black+4bp); dot(C,black+4bp);
label("$A=(0,0)$",A,SW,black);
label("$B=(14,0)$",B,SE,black);
label("$C=(5,12)$",C,N,black);
\end{asy}
\end{center}

\begin{solution}
The side lengths are $AB=14$, $BC=15$, $CA=13$; the semiperimeter is
$21$ and Heron's formula gives area $84$.

The tunnel is the incenter. Its distance from each road is
$r=84/21=4$. Since $AB$ is $y=0$, the incenter has $y=4$.
The line $AC$ is $12x-5y=0$, so its distance condition is
$|12x-20|/13=4$. Taking the solution inside the triangle gives
$\boxed{I=(6,4)}$, at distance $\boxed{4}$ from each road.

The prison is the circumcenter. The perpendicular bisector of $AB$
is $x=7$. Equating squared distances to $A$ and $C$ gives
$10x+24y=169$, hence $\boxed{O=(7,33/8)}$.
Its distance from every tower is
$\sqrt{7^2+(33/8)^2}=\boxed{65/8}$.

\begin{center}
\begin{asy}
size(6.5cm);
pair A=(0,0), B=(14,0), C=(5,12);
pair I=(6,4), O=(7,33/8);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,black+0.8bp);
dot(A,black+3bp); dot(B,black+3bp); dot(C,black+3bp);
label("$A$",A,SW,black); label("$B$",B,SE,black);
label("$C$",C,N,black);
dot(I,blue+5bp); dot(O,blue+5bp);
label("$I$",I,SW,blue);
label("$O$",O,NE,blue);
\end{asy}
\end{center}
\end{solution}
