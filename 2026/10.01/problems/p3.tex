Three of Varun's watchtowers occupy $A=(0,0)$, $B=(14,0)$, and $C=(5,12)$.
Commander Varun wants to know how many non-overlapping tanks, each with
area one square unit, will fit in the triangular region between the towers.
What number does he obtain?

\begin{center}
\begin{asy}
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0), B=(14,0), C=(5,12);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,black+0.9bp);
dot(A,black+4bp); dot(B,black+4bp); dot(C,black+4bp);
label("$A=(0,0)$",A,SW,black);
label("$B=(14,0)$",B,SE,black);
label("$C=(5,12)$",C,N,black);
\end{asy}
\end{center}

\begin{solution}
The base $AB$ has length $14$, and the altitude from $C$ to $AB$ is $12$.
Thus the area between the towers is
\[
\boxed{\frac12\cdot14\cdot12=84\text{ square units}}.
\]
The intended answer is $\boxed{84\text{ tanks}}$, treating this as an area
question. The statement specifies only each tank's area; actual packing
of rigid tanks would also depend on their shapes and dimensions.
\end{solution}
