Commander Varun's three watchtowers occupy A = (0, 0), B = (14, 0), and C = (5, 12). Commander Varun very evilley devises to figure out how many non-overlapping tanks with area 1 square unit will fit between the towers. What is the number Varun came up with?

\begin{solution}
The base $AB$ has length $14$, and the altitude from $C$ to $AB$ is $12$.
Thus the area between the towers is
\[
\boxed{\frac12\cdot14\cdot12=84\text{ square units}}.
\]
The intended answer is $\boxed{84\text{ tanks}}$, treating this as an area
question. The statement specifies only each tank's area; actual packing
of rigid tanks would also depend on their shapes and dimensions.
\end{solution}
