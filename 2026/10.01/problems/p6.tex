The final escape tunnel is a 2 $\times$ 9 grid of square rooms. Engineers can seal adjacent rooms with 1 $\times$ 2 rectangular barricades, placed either horizontally or vertically. Every room must be covered exactly once. How many different complete arrangements are possible?

\begin{solution}
Let $a_n$ be the number of tilings of a $2\times n$ passage. The first
column is either covered by one vertical domino, leaving a $2\times(n-1)$
passage, or by two horizontal dominoes, leaving a $2\times(n-2)$ passage.
Thus $a_n=a_{n-1}+a_{n-2}$, with $a_0=a_1=1$.
These are shifted Fibonacci numbers, so
$\boxed{a_9=55}.$
\end{solution}
