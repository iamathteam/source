Six frontier towns $A,B,C,D,E,F$ are each connected by one road to
the central fort $O$; there are no other existing roads. The rescue party
must start at $A$, finish at $B$, and traverse every road exactly once.
Varun's retreat destroyed two bridges, but the engineers can build exactly
two new roads between frontier towns $C,D,E,F$.
Which two should they build to minimize cost?

\begin{center}
\begin{asy}
size(5cm);
pair O=(0,0), A=(0,2), B=(1.73,1), C=(1.73,-1);
pair D=(0,-2), E=(-1.73,-1), F=(-1.73,1);
for (pair P : new pair[]{A,B,C,D,E,F}) {
  draw(O--P,black+0.85bp);
  dot(P,black+4bp);
}
dot(O,black+4bp);
label("$O$",O,W,black); label("$A$",A,N,black);
label("$B$",B,NE,black); label("$C$",C,SE,black);
label("$D$",D,S,black); label("$E$",E,SW,black);
label("$F$",F,NW,black);
\end{asy}
\end{center}

\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
\text{Proposed road}&CD&EF&CE&DF&CF&DE\\ \hline
\text{Cost}&9&5&4&3&6&8
\end{array}
\]

\begin{solution}
An Euler trail from $A$ to $B$ exists precisely when those are the only
two vertices of odd degree. Currently all six frontier towns have degree
one, and the fort has degree six. The two new roads must therefore pair
up $C,D,E,F$, raising each of their degrees to two.
The three possible pairings cost $9+5=14$, $4+3=7$, and $6+8=14$.
Build $\boxed{CE\text{ and }DF}$ at total cost $\boxed{7}$.
\end{solution}
