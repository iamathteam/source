The final escape tunnel is a $2\times20$ grid of square rooms. Engineers
can seal adjacent rooms with $1\times2$ rectangular barricades, placed
either horizontally or vertically. Every room must be covered exactly
once. How many different complete arrangements are possible?

\begin{center}
\begin{asy}
unitsize(0.8cm);
for (int i=0; i<=5; ++i) draw((i,0)--(i,2),black+0.75bp);
for (int j=0; j<=2; ++j) draw((0,j)--(5,j),black+0.75bp);
label("$\cdots$",(5.6,1),E,black);
\end{asy}
\end{center}

\begin{solution}
Let $a_n$ be the number of tilings of a $2\times n$ passage. The first
column is either covered by one vertical domino, leaving a $2\times(n-1)$
passage, or by two horizontal dominoes, leaving a $2\times(n-2)$ passage.
Thus $a_n=a_{n-1}+a_{n-2}$, with $a_0=a_1=1$.
These are shifted Fibonacci numbers, so
$\boxed{a_{20}=10{,}946}$.
\end{solution}
