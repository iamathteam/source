Martin learns of new information that someone on his side betrayed him and stole his sword the MAGICALibur along with his Precious Ring.

Jannik Sinner: ``Gollum stole the sword.''

Gollum: ``Darth Vader stole the sword.''

Loki: ``I did not steal the sword.''

Darth Vader: ``Gollum is lying.''

Exactly one person stole the sword, and exactly one of the four statements is a lie. Who stole the trophy?

\begin{solution}
Gollum's and Darth Vader's statements are negations of each other, so
exactly one of those two statements is false. The other two statements
must both be true. Jannik's true statement identifies
$\boxed{\text{Gollum}}$ as the thief. Then Gollum's statement is the sole
lie, while Loki and Darth Vader tell the truth.
The PDF's final reference to a ``trophy'' is understood to mean the stolen sword.
\end{solution}
