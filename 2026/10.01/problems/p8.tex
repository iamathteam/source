Martin glimpses an analog clock. The smaller angle between the hands is
$89^\circ$, and the hour hand is just past $7$. Find the exact time of day.

\begin{solution}
Let $t$ be the number of minutes after $7{:}00$. The hour hand is at
$210+t/2$ degrees clockwise from $12$, and the minute hand is at $6t$ degrees.
For $0\le t<60$, the smaller angle is $89^\circ$ precisely when
\[
210-\frac{11}{2}t=89\quad\text{or}\quad
210-\frac{11}{2}t=-89.
\]
Thus $t=22$ or $t=598/11=54+4/11$. The possible times are
\[
\boxed{7{:}22}\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\boxed{7{:}54{:}21\tfrac{9}{11}}.
\]
``Just past $7$'' favors the intended answer $\boxed{7{:}22}$, since at
that time the hour hand is only $11^\circ$ past $7$. Without a precise
meaning for ``just past,'' the second time cannot be excluded mathematically.
The clock alone also does not determine a.m. versus p.m.
\end{solution}
