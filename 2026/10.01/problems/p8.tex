He was running out of time, he could see the sunlight up the sky, so he put his horse in overdrive, Marty. The city was very cold and empty and he could just barely glance at the analog clock before being blinded by the light. He could see that the angle between the hands was 89 degrees and the hour hand just past 7. Find the exact time of day.

\begin{solution}
Let $t$ be the number of minutes after $7{:}00$. The hour hand is at
$210+t/2$ degrees clockwise from $12$, and the minute hand is at $6t$ degrees.
For the intended configuration,
\[
210+\frac{t}{2}-6t=89,
\qquad \frac{11}{2}t=121,
\qquad t=22.
\]
The intended answer is $\boxed{7{:}22}$.
\end{solution}
