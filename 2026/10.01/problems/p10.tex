Martin had taken a big kick from Marty* to the head when he was trying to setup his 2-seater horse. His horseodometer (in meters) started at 0 and ended at the number of pages in a diary of a wimpy book squared. The he realized he Forgot About Dre when Martin was getting on Marty while Dr. Dre was resting. If he goes back to get Dre and takes him to a tavern 500 km away, what number would his horseodometer show?

*Marty is Prince Martin's royal horse, the horse he was gifted for his 8th birthday. Royal horses can hit supersonic speeds.

\begin{solution}
The intended page count is $217$, so Martin first travels
$217^2=47{,}089$ meters. Returning to pick up Dre adds another
$47{,}089$ meters, and the trip from the pickup point to the tavern adds
$500{,}000$ meters. The horseodometer records total distance traveled:
\[
47{,}089+47{,}089+500{,}000=\boxed{594{,}178\text{ meters}}.
\]
\end{solution}
