Martin's horseodometer, measured in meters, starts at $0$ and reaches
the square of the number of pages in a Diary of a Wimpy Kid book.
He realizes he forgot Dr. Dre, returns to get him, and then takes him to
a tavern $500$ km away. What number does the horseodometer show?

\begin{solution}
Let $p$ be the book's page count. Assume Martin retraces his route to
Dre, and the tavern is $500$ km from that pickup point. An odometer
records total distance traveled, including the return journey. Its reading is
\[
p^2+p^2+500\cdot1000=\boxed{500{,}000+2p^2\text{ meters}}.
\]
The PDF does not specify a title, edition, or page count, so it does not
determine a unique numerical answer. For example, if the intended count
is $p=217$, the reading is $\boxed{594{,}178\text{ meters}}$.
\end{solution}
