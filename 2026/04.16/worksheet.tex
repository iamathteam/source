\vspace{.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2cm]
    \item If $a+b=3$ and $a^2+b^2=89$, what is the value of $a^3+b^3$?
    \item How many 3-digit integers have digits in strictly increasing order?
    \item How many positive integers less than 1000 are divisible by 3 or 5 but not both?
    \item The roots of $x^2 - x + 6 = 0$ are $a$ and $b$. What is $a^2 + b^2$?
    \item Given that $x$ and $y$ are distinct nonzero real numbers such that $x+\tfrac{2}{x} = y + \tfrac{2}{y}$, what is $xy$? 
    \item Peppa Pig Plays Poker impatiently. She stupidly always folds if she does not posses a pair in her paw. What is the probablity Peppa Pig does not fold? Assume that Peppa Pig is dealt two cards randomly and without replacement from a standard 52 card deck.
    \item Let $S$ be the set of positive integers $n$ for which $\tfrac{1}{n}$ has the repeating decimal representation $0.\overline{ab} = 0.ababab\cdots,$ with $a$ and $b$ different digits. What is the sum of the elements of $S$? 
    \item The sequence $\log_{12}{162}$, $\log_{12}{x}$, $\log_{12}{y}$, $\log_{12}{z}$, $\log_{12}{1250}$ is an arithmetic progression. What is $x$? 
\end{enumerate}
