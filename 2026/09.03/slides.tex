\begin{frame}[t]{Question 1}
    This is the last digit of $67^{76}$ and it may be the number of shots you have in Eminem's Lose Yourself.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{Question 2}
    Detroit Lions head coach Dan Campbell has this many ways to choose 2 captains from a 53-man roster.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{Question 3}
    This is the number of Diary of a Wimpy Kid books - or the number of coin flips it would take to have more than a million different possible results.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{Question 4}
    Jared Goff is in a very quick Ferrari. However, his team is against him and are messing with his tires. He has 5 different tires: Soft, Medium, Hard, Intermediates and Wet tires. If the team must use 4 of those tires to place into 4 different wheel locations, how many ways can Ferrari mess with Jared?
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{Question 5}
    It takes Rohan's factory is able to produce weird uniformly distributed mass tennis balls of radius 3 inches each with a mass of 108 grams. He now wants tennis balls of same density with a radius of 5 inches. What is the new mass of the ball? 
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{Question 6}
    Suppose we have a typical $8\times 8$ chessboard, and remove two of the corners. Is it possible to tile the remaining of the chessboard with dominoes?
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}[t]{Question 7}
    Brilliant Ben Shelton hits a serve at 225 kilometers per hour, while  Slow Sreyas Sabbani hits a serve at 10 kilometers per hour. What percent faster is Ben’s serve than Sreyas’s?
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{Question 8}
     Rohan, Sreyas, and Marty are prisoners each wearing one hat that is either yellow or pink. At one moment, each person chooses to either guess the color of their hat or stay silent. If at least one person guesses their hat color correctly, and no one guesses wrong, then they are released. Otherwise, they are sentenced to death. Find an optimal strategy so the three prisoners can hopefully survive.
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}[t]{Question 9}
    What is the units digit of \[ \underbrace{2027^{2027^{\cdot^{\cdot^{\cdot^{2027}}}}}}_{\text{2027 times}}\]
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}[t]{Question 10}
    Aidan and Hutchinson flip coins. They flip coins until they get one head. What is the probability that Aidan flips the coin less times than Hutchinson?
\end{frame}
