\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=4cm]
    \item In an intense Math Team Competition Timothy was against Elizabeth in a dice game. They each rolled two dice and whoever had the higher sum would win. However they had funny dice and each dice had 8 sides numbered 1-8 with equal probability of the dice landing on each side. What is the probability that they both tie?
    \item In the West Hangar, there were 9 different one seater fighter jets. Naz, Hanrui and Sunwoo want to each buy 3 jets. How many ways can this be done? 
    \item A swim team at IA is filling a pool that holds 36,000 gallons of water. When Sahas, Fadzi, and Aarav arrive, the pool is already half full.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Sahas can fill the pool from empty to full in 6 hours.
        \item Fadzi can fill the pool from empty to full in 4 hours.
        \item Aarav can fill the pool from empty to full in 12 hours.
    \end{itemize}
    If all three work at the same time, how long will it take to fill the pool completely from its current level?

    \pagebreak
    \item During math team Rebanto and Ishan are betting online (during club time) on a soccer match between FC Barcelona and Real Madrid. Barcelona is twice as likely to win as Real Madrid, and there are no draws. Rebanto bets \$500 on Barcelona, while Ishan bets \$1000 on Real Madrid. Assuming there are no sportsbook fees, what is the expected total amount of money they will receive combined after the match? For example: if Marty bet 40 dollars on Barcelona, if Barcelona win, Marty gets 60 dollars and if they lose he gets no money.
    \vspace{-2cm}
    \item Aaryan and Bhanu are terribly bored during Club A and Club C respectively. They remembered that they love Math Team and decided they want to manually calculate as many factorials as they can. They each start at one. If Aaryan gets all the way to 410 and Bhanu gets to 676, how many more trailing 0's does Bhanu have than Aaryan?
    \item Mihir tried multiplying two numbers $a$ and $b$, but he accidentally reversed the digits of $a$. When Arshia saw Mihir's result was $161$, she knew something was wrong. What is the correct value of $a\times b$?
    \item Amog makes a paper airplane, and he claims it flies faster than the Concorde. However, Vaibhav had his suspicions about Amog. To test Amog's airplane,  he must verify whether $v(t)>686$. If Amog's airplane reaches the required speed, please let Vaibhav know at what time $t$ this happens.
    $$v(t)=2t^3-42t^2+294t$$
    \item Jeremy and Sophia are painting Rubik's cubes. Specifically, Jeremy only paints the corner cubes (3 faces) and the edge cubes (2 faces), while Sophia paints the rest of the cubes (which all have 1 face). For a $3\times3\times 3$ cube, Jeremy will paint more faces than Sophia. For what value of $n$ will Sophia paint more faces in a $n\times n\times n$ cube than Jeremy?
\end{enumerate}
